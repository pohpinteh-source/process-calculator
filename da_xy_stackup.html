📖 Quick Guide

Calculator Function
  • Calculate the remaining gap between chips or cavity after tolerance study.
  • Evaluate the impact of glue smear on available chip-to-chip or chip-to-cavity clearance.
  • Support both G1 and G2 gap analysis conditions.
  • Automatically determine whether the design condition is Risk or No Risk.
  • Provide early assessment of potential chip interference issues before production.
  • Support package design verification and tolerance stack-up analysis.
  1. Select G1 or G2 mode.
  2. Refer to the drawing image.
  3. Input Gap between chips / cavity in drawing.
  4. Input Glue Smear at the selected area.
  5. Only enter values in yellow highlighted cells.
  6. Gap after tolerance study will be calculated automatically.
  7. No Risk = Sufficient Gap.
  8. Risk = Possible Interference.
  9. Remark: Assume both chips have the same BLT.
  10. Click Clear to reset all values.
