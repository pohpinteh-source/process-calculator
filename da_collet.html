📖 Quick Guide

Calculator Function
  • Calculate the minimum gap between Collet (ØA) and cavity wall or adjacent chip.
  • Evaluate the risk of collet interference during die attach placement.
  • Consider chip size, chip thickness, cavity geometry, collet diameter and placement accuracy.
  • Automatically determine whether the design condition is Risk or No Risk.
  • Support collet selection and package design verification before production release.
  • Help prevent collet collision, chip edge contact and placement-related defects.
  1. Refer to the DA Collet diagram shown above.
  2. Enter Gap between Chips / Cavity (G).
  3. Enter Height of Cavity / Adjacent Chip (H).
  4. Enter Chip Thickness (CT).
  5. Enter Chip Size.
  6. Enter Collet OD and Collet Diameter (A).
  7. Enter Collet MTA.
  8. Enter Placement Accuracy.
  9. Gap of Collet (ØA) Hit Cavity Wall / Adjacent Chip (X) will be calculated automatically.
  10. Result ≥ 0 = No Risk.
  11. Result < 0 = Risk of Collet hitting cavity wall or adjacent chip.
  12. Click Clear to reset all values.
  13. Click Back to return to Home Page.
