📖 Quick Guide

Calculator Function
  • Analyze US Scan result files (CSV, TXT, XLS and XLSX).
  • Determine Green Box Qty and FAIL Qty based on selected Void Spec.
  • Identify Pad Type (1 Pad or 2 Pads).
  • Generate Yield Analysis Report in XLSX format.
  1. Upload a CSV, TXT, XLS or XLSX US Scan result file.
  2. For standard US Scan format, keep "Swap Row / Column" checked.
  3. Panel Incoming Reject = Total Qty Per Panel − DA In or DA Out Qty (eMOS)
  4. US Scan Out Qty = DA In or DA Out Qty (eMOS) − FAIL Qty
  5. US Scan Yield (%) = (US Scan Out Qty ÷ DA In or DA Out Qty (eMOS)) × 100
  6. Set Void Spec according to product requirement (default = 20%).
  7. Select comparison operator:
    • > = Reject when Void % exceeds Spec.
    • ≥ = Reject when Void % equals or exceeds Spec.
  8. Pad Type detection:
    • 🟢 1 Pad (1 Box)
    • 🔴 2 Pads (2 Boxes)
  9. Export XLSX generates:
    • Raw Data Sheet, Yield Summary Sheet, Void % Analysis Result
  10. Click Clear to start a new analysis.
  11. Click Back to return to Home Page.
