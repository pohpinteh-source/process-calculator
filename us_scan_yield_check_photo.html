📖 Quick Guide

Calculator Function
  • Analyze US Scan photo and automatically detect Row Qty and Column Qty.
  • Automatically identify Green Box Qty and FAIL Qty (Red Box) from the US Scan image.
  • Provide automated Yield (%) calculation for production verification.
  • Support comparison and validation between US Scan map quantities and DA In Qty recorded in eMOS.
  1. Upload US Scan image. (BMP, PNG, JPG or JPEG image file)
  2. The system automatically detects:
    • Row & Col Qty
    • Total Qty Per Panel, Green Box Qty & FAIL Qty (Red Box)
  3. Enter DA In or DA Out Qty (eMOS) from eMOS.
  4. Panel Incoming Reject = Total Qty Per Panel − DA In or DA Out Qty (eMOS)
  5. US Scan Out Qty (eMOS) = DA In or DA Out Qty (eMOS) − FAIL Qty (Red Box)
  6. US Scan Yield % = ( DA In or DA Out Qty (eMOS) − FAIL Qty) ÷ DA In or DA Out Qty (eMOS) × 100
  7. If detected quantities do not match the wafer map and eMOS data, verify the image quality and eMOS input value.
  8. Click Clear to analyse a new image.
  9. Click Back to return to Home Page.
