Chip Counter

📖 Quick Guide

  1. Click 📁 Select Image or 📷 Take Photo.
  2. Use a clear image with minimum reflection.
  3. Drag and draw a box around the chip area.
  4. Release the mouse to crop the image.
  5. Detection runs automatically after crop.
  6. Adjust Threshold until chips appear correctly.
  7. Adjust Min Blob Area to remove noise.
  8. Verify every chip has a red dot.
  9. Check Qty result on the right panel.
  10. Click New Image to start a new analysis.
