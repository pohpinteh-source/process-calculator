📖 Quick Guide

Purpose
  • Check BOOP risk and available design margin.
  • Evaluate process capability using production data (optional).

How to Use
  1. Enter BPO, BOOP, Ball Diameter and Ball Tolerance.
  2. Enter Bond Accuracy and Operator Teaching variation.
  3. Click Calculate and review Part A - Design Assessment.
  4. Optionally enter Mean Ball Diameter and Ball Size Sigma (1σ).
  5. Click Calculate again and review Part B - Statistical Assessment.
Result Interpretation
  • ✅ PASS = No BOOP risk predicted.
  • ⚠️ WARNING = Margin is limited.
  • ❌ FAIL = Design improvement required.
  • Higher design margin and higher process capability indicate lower BOOP risk.
  • Higher σ capability = Lower BOOP risk.
  • Part A (Design Assessment) can be used without production data.
  • Part B (Statistical Assessment) requires Mean Ball Diameter and Ball Size Sigma.

📘 Calculation Method

Part A – Design Assessment (Worst Case Analysis)
Min BPO = Nominal BPO − BPO Tolerance
Maximum Allowable Ball Diameter = Min BPO + (2 × Allowable BOOP) − Total Design Tolerance
Remaining Margin = Maximum Allowable Ball Diameter − Ball Max Diameter
Design Status:
  • ✅ PASS → Margin > 5 µm
  • ⚠️ WARNING → Margin 0 to 5 µm
  • ❌ FAIL → Margin < 0 µm
Part B – Statistical Assessment (Process Capability)
Total RSS Sigma = √( Ball Sigma² + Placement Sigma² + Teaching Sigma² )
Process Capability (Z) = (Bondable Area − Mean Ball Diameter) / Total RSS Sigma
Predicted BOOP Risk (PPM) = (1 − NormalCDF(Z)) × 1,000,000
